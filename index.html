Accessible Learning Management System: Core Learner Interfaces

Designed core learner and educator interfaces for an accessibility-first LMS tailored for Deaf, Hard-of-Hearing, and visually impaired users ahead of product release.

Problem & Focus

  • Traditional educational tools feature dense hierarchies and unlabelled interactions that fail screen reader and switch device navigation.
  • Focused on creating clean, predictable learner flows with strict keyboard focus visibility and zero color-only dependency.

UX Documentation & Design Systems

  • Documented core learner screens (Lesson Hub, Assessment Review, Progress Tracker) and standardized Figma design tokens.
  • Integrated qualitative feedback synthesized through Claude and Multi to rapidly adapt user flows to accessibility needs.

Assistive Testing & Validation

  • Conducted usability sessions with screen reader users (NVDA/JAWS) and sign-language interpreter workflows.
  • Verified WCAG 2.2 contrast ratios (7:1+), accessible modal focus traps, and intuitive touch target boundaries.

Assistive Language Platform: AI-Powered Visual Learning Assets

Designed an in-development language acquisition platform for deaf students, replacing phonics-dependent exercises with high-fidelity visual learning pathways.

The Challenge

  • Standard language platforms depend heavily on audio cues, leaving deaf learners without intuitive anchors.
  • Students require sharp, unambiguous visual cues and contextual illustrations to connect abstract vocabulary to concepts.

Magnific & Prompt Workflow

  • Formulated prompt templates for concept representations and leveraged Magnific to upscale and refine visual assets.
  • Eliminated AI generation blur and artifacts, ensuring visual symbols render crisp and accessible on high-density displays.

Interaction Prototyping

  • Explored progressive disclosure exercises in Claude Design and Figma to validate touch gestures and positive visual feedback states.
